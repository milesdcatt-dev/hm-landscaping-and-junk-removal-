05Questions

Hot Tub Removal questions

Do I need to drain the hot tub first?

Yes, please drain it and turn off its power at the breaker before we come. If you are not sure how, say so in your text.

Can you get it out of a fenced backyard?

Usually, yes. Send a photo of the gate and the path to the street with your tub photos so the quote covers it.

Can you take the deck or pad under it too?

Yes. Tell us if you want the deck or patio pieces gone as well, and we will price it all as one job.

How much does hot tub removal cost?

It is priced by the job: the size of the tub, where it sits and how hard the path out is. Send a photo and you get one clear number back.
