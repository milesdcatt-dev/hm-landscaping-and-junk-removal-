Privacy Policy

What we collect when you ask for a quote, and how we use it. Short version: only to get back to you about your job.

Last updated: November 27, 2026

H&M Junk Removal & Landscaping is operated by Miles Dickey in Northern Virginia. This page explains what we collect when you contact us through hmjunkland.org, by phone or by text, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you send a quote request on this site, the form collects:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your email address, if you choose to give it (on the full quote form)
  • Your town, the service you need, and any details you type about the job
  • A photo of the job, if you choose to attach one
  • How you would like us to reach you (text, call or email)
  • Whether you checked the box agreeing to get texts about your quote

If you call or text us, we get your phone number and whatever you send us, such as photos of the job.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to you about your job: to give you a quote, answer your questions, and schedule and do the work. We do not sell your information, and we do not rent or trade it.

Where it is stored

This website is hosted on Netlify. Quote form submissions, including any photo you attach, are stored with Netlify Forms. Texts and calls go through our phone and texting providers so the messages can be delivered. These companies handle the information only to provide those services to us.

The site also uses a Google tag (Google Ads) that can set cookies to measure visits and whether someone sent a quote request. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings.

Text messages (SMS)

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We only text you about your quote and job if you opted in, for example by checking the text box on our quote form, or if you called or texted us first. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. See our Terms & Conditions for the full SMS terms.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent us. Call or text (202) 999-7885 and we will take care of it.
